Eazify AOV helps you increase average order value with on-store offers your customers see right on the product page: volume discounts, fixed product bundles, "frequently bought together" suggestions, and build-your-own mix and match bundles. Every offer type is fully customizable to match your theme, and built-in analytics show you exactly how much extra revenue each offer generates.

## What you can do

- **Create offers without writing code.** Every offer type is configured from the Eazify AOV admin — pick products or collections, set your discount, and customize the look.
- **Match your theme.** Colors, fonts, borders, badges, and layout are all configurable per offer, so widgets look native to your storefront instead of bolted on.
- **Add urgency and incentives.** Countdown timers and free-gift add-ons are available on supported offer types to encourage customers to act now.
- **See what's working.** The [Analytics](/analytics) page tracks views, add-to-carts, orders, and the additional revenue each offer generates, so you know which offers to keep and which to retire.
