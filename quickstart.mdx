This guide takes you from installing Eazify AOV to seeing a live offer on your storefront.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you must have:

- A Shopify store you're an admin on
- At least one product (or collection) you want to promote with an offer

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Install Eazify AOV from the Shopify App Store. You'll be redirected to your Shopify admin to approve the requested permissions, then dropped into the Eazify AOV dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    During onboarding you'll see the available plans. You can start on the Free plan and upgrade at any time — see [Plans](/plans) for what each tier includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first offer">
    From the dashboard, click **Create offer** and pick an offer type:

    - [Volume discount](/offers/volume-discount) for "buy more, save more" quantity tiers
    - [Product bundle](/offers/product-bundle) for a fixed set of products sold together
    - [Frequently bought together](/offers/frequently-bought-together) for complementary product suggestions
    - [Mix and match](/offers/mix-and-match) for a build-your-own bundle

    Pick which products or collections the offer applies to, set your discount, then customize the design so it matches your theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the offer">
    Save the offer as a draft while you're testing it, then switch it to **Published** once you're happy with it. Draft offers are never shown to customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the widget on your theme">
    Eazify AOV renders offers through a theme app embed, which needs to be turned on once per theme:

    1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes**.
    2. Click **Customize** on your live theme.
    3. Open **App embeds** (usually in the left sidebar) and turn on **Eazify AOV**.
    4. Save.

    If you don't see the offer on your storefront after publishing it, this is the most common reason — the app embed needs to be turned on for every theme you use.
  </Step>
</Steps>
