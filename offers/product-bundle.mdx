title: "Product bundle"
description: "Sell a fixed set of products together as one discounted bundle"
---

Product bundle groups a fixed set of products into a single offer — customers add the whole bundle to their cart in one click, at a discounted price. Use it for curated kits ("starter kit", "gift set") where you want to control exactly which products are bundled, rather than letting the customer choose.

## Building a bundle

1. **Add products.** Pick each product that belongs in the bundle. For products with variants, choose whether the offer applies to all variants or specific ones.
2. **Set quantities.** Each product in the bundle can require more than one unit.
3. **Set the discount.** Apply a bundle-wide discount as a percentage, a fixed amount off, or leave it at the regular combined price (`Default`).
4. **Write the call-to-action.** Customize the add-to-cart button text (for example "Get this bundle") and the label shown while it's adding to cart.

The bundle gets its own product page on your storefront (with its own URL), showing every item included, the combined price, and the savings.

## Options

- **Countdown timer** — same countdown component available on other offer types, to add urgency.
- **Show related products** — optionally show other products related to the bundle below it, so customers can keep browsing after viewing the bundle.
- **Redirect to checkout** — send customers straight to checkout when they add the bundle, instead of the cart page, to shorten the path to purchase.
- **Bundle image** — use a custom image for the bundle product page instead of defaulting to the first product's image.
- **Quantity input** — let customers adjust the quantity of the whole bundle before adding it to cart.

## Design

Colors, fonts, borders, corner radius, and badge style are all configurable — see [Design customization](/design-customization) for details that apply across every offer type.
