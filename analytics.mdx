title: "Analytics"
description: "Track visitors, orders, and the extra revenue your offers generate"
---

The Analytics page shows exactly how your offers are performing, so you can tell which ones are earning their place on your storefront. All numbers only count activity that went through an Eazify AOV offer — pick a date range at the top of the page to change the period.

## Summary cards

- **Visitors** — how many times an offer widget was viewed.
- **Total orders** — how many orders included at least one Eazify AOV offer.
- **Total Revenue** — total revenue from those orders.
- **Additional revenue** — the extra revenue your offers generated on top of the base purchase, attributed specifically to the discount/bundle mechanic.

## Views, add to cart & orders

A daily chart of three funnel stages — views, add-to-carts, and orders — for the selected date range, plus an overall **conversion rate** (the share of viewers who went on to purchase). Use this to spot whether an underperforming offer is a visibility problem (low views), a persuasion problem (views but few add-to-carts), or a checkout problem (add-to-carts but few completed orders).

## Bundle performance

A breakdown by offer, showing:

- **Added to cart** — how many times the offer was added to cart
- **Total orders** — how many orders included it
- **Additional revenue** — extra revenue generated by that specific offer

Sort by additional revenue to quickly find your best and worst performing offers. Use the search box to find a specific offer by name.

## Recent bundle orders

A list of the individual orders that included an Eazify AOV offer, with the order number, date, offer name, and additional revenue for that order. Click an order number to open it in your Shopify admin.

<Note>
  If a customer refunds or cancels an order, the revenue attributed to it is automatically removed from these numbers — you don't need to adjust anything manually.
</Note>
