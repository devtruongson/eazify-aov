Eazify AOV starts on a **Free** plan with no time limit, so you can set up offers and see how they perform before deciding whether to upgrade. Paid plans raise the limits on things like revenue processed through your offers and unlock additional features.

For current pricing and exactly what's included in each plan, open **Pricing** from the Eazify AOV navigation in your Shopify admin — plans and limits are subject to change, so the in-app page is always the source of truth.

## Founding member: Lifetime access

For a limited number of stores, Eazify AOV offers **Lifetime** access — all paid features, free for life, with no recurring subscription. This offer is capped to a limited number of spots and is not guaranteed to be available.

If you see the founding member banner in your dashboard, click it to claim your spot. Claiming submits your request for review — it doesn't upgrade your account automatically. Once approved, your store is moved to the Lifetime plan and the banner won't show again.

<Tip>
  Already claimed but don't see Lifetime features yet? Reach out through **Help center** in the Eazify AOV navigation and we'll confirm the status of your claim.
</Tip>
